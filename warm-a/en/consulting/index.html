Why go gluten-free now.

Gluten-free is not a "special accommodation." It is a business strategy that ties directly to sales, foot traffic and your brand. It starts with the right knowledge and doing it properly.

A direct answer to inbound demand

Many visitors to Japan look for gluten-free options. With the right knowledge and a well-prepared gluten-free menu and service, you meet that demand head-on.

More sales, new customers

Gluten-free opens the door to customers you could not reach before. As diets diversify, it is a concrete way to grow your sales.

Avoid risk with the right knowledge

Calling something "gluten-free" takes accurate knowledge based on international standards. Mistakes in labeling or handling damage trust in your brand. Building that knowledge inside your company is the foundation for everything else.

GFCO certification: trust and differentiation overseas

With GFCO certification, we help you earn the trust of overseas buyers and set your products apart in overseas markets.

Get ahead of the market with product development

Japan's gluten-free market is still developing. Enter now and you can establish your brand while competition is limited. With more than 700 recipes of experience, we help you make products that sell.
