About the tempura batter mix

It suits a wide range of diets, such as additive-free, gluten-free, vegan and halal.

It absorbs about 40% less oil than wheat-based tempura flour. Draft

How to use Draft

  1. Mix 70 g of the batter mix with 15 g of oil and 60–70 ml of water or sparkling water.
  2. Coat your ingredients and fry at 170°C or higher.
  3. Serve with salt or soy sauce.

You can flavour the batter with matcha, curry powder, sesame, cheese, yukari, aonori, cornflakes and more, or dust the mix straight onto ingredients and fry.
